Go Dutch with your friends.

Split a dinner, a trip or a flat — and always know who owes what.

Download on the App Store View the source

Everyone's in, in about a minute.

You're at the table, the bill has arrived, and nobody wants to install an app and make an account before the coffee gets cold.

So Dutch doesn't have accounts. Show your group's QR code, everyone scans it, and they're in — with the full app, on the free tier, forever. Joining someone else's group is always free and always unlimited.

Every group also gets a short word sequence like green-moon-tea, so two people can check they've landed in the same place.

What it does

Enough to settle a two-week trip for six people. Not much more than that.

Who owes whom

Not just a running total — the shortest list of payments that squares everybody up. Mark one paid and the balances clear.

Any currency

Paid in yen on a trip priced in euros? Enter the rate you actually got. It converts once, so the balance never drifts afterwards.

Uneven splits

Split a bill evenly, leave someone out, or give one person a double share. Every remaining cent is distributed, never dropped.

Live for everyone

Add an expense and it appears on everybody's phone. Shared through CloudKit, so there's no server in the middle to trust or pay for.

Siri and Shortcuts

"Add an expense to Berlin Trip." Ask Siri what you owe in a group, or build the whole thing into a Shortcut of your own.

Spotlight and quick actions

Search your groups straight from the Home Screen, or long-press the icon to add an expense to the one you were last in.

Small, and entirely yours.

Dutch has no backend. There is no server that holds your expenses, no account to create, and no analytics of any kind. Your groups live in your iCloud, and sharing a group is Apple's own CloudKit sharing — the same mechanism as a shared photo album.

That isn't a policy that could change next quarter. There is nowhere for the data to go, because the infrastructure to collect it was never built.

Free for one group. $4.99 for as many as you like.

Everything in Dutch works on the free tier — every feature, no ads, no trial. The one limit is that you can create one group at a time. Groups you join don't count, so scanning a friend's code is always free however many times you do it.

If you're the one starting the trips, a single purchase lifts the limit for good. One payment, not a subscription, shared with your Family Sharing group.

The source is open under MPL 2.0 — build it yourself and the limit isn't there at all. What the App Store version buys you is not having to.
